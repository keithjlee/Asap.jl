# Asap v1.5.0

Load cases on the differentiable path, interior cut lines in polygon meshes, and a dapped-end
walkthrough. This release only adds API: existing models, the single-case pure path and the stateful
case machinery (`solve_cases!`, `combine`, `envelope`) are unchanged.

## Added

### Load cases on the differentiable path
- `case_loads(model; cases)` builds a `CaseLoads` table once: per-case nodal loads, the fixed-end
  forces that do not follow the design (`VariableElement` loads), the frozen load vectors, and the
  element loads of every case. Each load's scatter rows sit in its own case's block, so the
  design-dependent fixed-end forces of all cases come out of one sparse product per load kind. The
  cache is left exactly as it was.
- `solve(model, state, cl)`: every case's displacements (`n_dof × n_cases`) from ONE stiffness
  assembly and factorization. `compliance(model, state, cl)`: the per-case compliances. Every case's
  element loads (frame line and point loads and self-weight, quadrilateral and triangle shell surface
  loads) are re-evaluated at the design (`loads = :design`) or kept at the model's state (`:frozen`).
  Rigid diaphragms are supported.
- `combination_weights(cl, combo)`: a `LoadCombination` as column weights λ, so `U * λ` is the
  combination's displacement; a misspelled case throws. (A combination's compliance is
  `(Fλ)ᵀ(Uλ)`, not a weighted sum of case compliances.)
- `case_Pf(cl, state)`: the per-case fixed-end forces; `fef_local(cl, state)`: the per-load local
  fixed-end forces for per-case force recovery.
- Differentiable with Zygote (linear-cost rules: a gradient stays about 25× the primal from 33 to
  456 elements and 66 to 912 loads), ForwardDiff, Enzyme (reverse and forward) and Mooncake. The
  Enzyme matrix gains a `cases` scenario.
- Verified against `solve_cases!`, `combine` and one single-case solve per case at perturbed
  designs; the load-cases guide gains a section on the differentiable path.

### Meshing and disturbed regions
- `mesh_polygon(…; segments)`: interior lines the element edges must follow, given as point pairs
  `(a, b)`: section cuts through the material, interior load or bearing lines. Their nodes come
  from `nodes_on_segment`, so `section_cut` integrates the exact force across any chosen line. A
  segment may end on the outline, a hole or another segment; a segment leaving the material, or
  crossing an edge or another segment, is refused.
- Walkthrough `examples/shells/07_dapped_end.jl` and its PDF report: a dapped-end beam. Bearing pads
  as pressures with rigid-body supports only (a self-equilibrated load set), exact interface cut
  forces, the nib tie and the hanger as integrated cut-force profiles (they converge under
  refinement), the crack direction at the re-entrant corner, and the diverging corner peak. The
  shells guide covers cut lines and flexible bearings.

## Known limitation
- `VariableElement` stiffness is not differentiable with Zygote (it mutates an array); ForwardDiff
  and Enzyme work. Unchanged from earlier releases.
