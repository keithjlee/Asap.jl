# Asap v1.4.0

Triangular shells, general planar meshes and the pieces a disturbed-region (strut-and-tie)
analysis needs. This release only adds API. Existing quadrilateral, frame and truss models give the
same results as v1.3.0: the shell walkthroughs' recorded results are bitwise identical, and the
only differences anywhere are rounding in the last digit of a truss sum and of one Zygote gradient
entry.

## Added

### Triangular shells
- `ShellElement` with 3 nodes, mixing freely with quadrilaterals, frames and trusses:
  - **Membrane:** Felippa's ANDES-OPT drilling triangle (2003), the optimal 3-node element with a
    corner drilling rotation: exact in-plane pure bending of rectangular two-triangle units at any
    aspect ratio. Its higher-order scaling `β₀` follows the membrane rigidity, so cracked and
    orthotropic `ShellRigiditySection`s are handled (`andes_beta0`).
  - **Plate:** MITC3+ (Lee, Lee & Bathe 2014): Reissner–Mindlin with a condensed cubic rotation
    bubble and the MITC3+ assumed transverse shear, so triangles and MITC4 quadrilaterals share
    one plate theory and triangles report transverse shear `Q`.
  - Verified against the primary sources (Felippa's worked example and the MITC3+ single-element
    eigenvalues, both to 5 digits) and on the standard benchmarks with triangulated meshes: Cook's
    membrane (1 %), thin square plates (2–3 %), the Scordelis–Lo roof (3 % at 16 × 16).
- Kernels: `andes_membrane_stiffness`, `andes_beta0`, `mitc3p_plate_stiffness`; `shell_frame` and
  `shell_stiffness` take 3 nodes.
- Surface loads (`AreaLoad`, `SelfWeight`), `shell_resultants` (at triangular coordinates
  `at = (r, s)`), `section_cut`, `shell_envelope` and `ShellGeo` cover triangles. `ShellGeo` keeps
  a triangle as a degenerate quad; `shell_faces` / `shell_face_owner` give triangles for plotting.
- `edge_loads` on a triangle edge adds the drilling moments the ANDES membrane needs,
  `∓α_b·(w·n)·l²/12` with α_b = 3/2, so a uniform traction reproduces a uniform stress exactly.

### General planar meshes
- `AbstractShellMesh`, the supertype of `WallMesh` and the new `ShellMesh` (nodes, elements,
  in-plane or 3D coordinates, boundary, named node and element groups).
- `shell_mesh(points, cells, section)`: elements from any mesher's output (triangles and
  quadrilaterals, orientation normalized, boundary loops recovered).
- `mesh_polygon(outline, section; holes, h, min_angle, points, seed, reuse)` (load
  DelaunayTriangulation.jl; new extension `AsapDelaunayExt`): constrained Delaunay with quality
  refinement of a polygon with polygonal holes. Holes may touch the outline or each other (at a
  vertex, a vertex on an edge, or along an edge: notches and adjacent openings); crossing or
  overlapping holes are refused. Every input edge's nodes are tracked (`boundary_nodes`), meshes
  are reproducible (`seed`), and existing nodes in the panel become mesh vertices (`reuse`).
- `read_gmsh(path, section)`: a dependency-free reader for Gmsh MSH 4.1 ASCII files. Physical
  groups become named node and element sets (`group_nodes`, `group_elements`); planar meshes keep
  in-plane coordinates, curved surfaces are read in 3D.
- `shell_mesh(...; reuse)`: a mesh node at an existing node's position is that node, as in
  `mesh_wall`.

### Disturbed regions
- `nodes_on_segment`, `edge_loads(mesh, a, b, w)`, `elements_in`, `total_reaction`,
  `circle_polygon`, and `fixnode!(node, ::Vector{Bool})` place bearings and loads on boundary
  segments and read reactions per bearing.
- Walkthrough `examples/shells/06_d_region.jl` and its PDF report: a deep transfer panel with a
  duct and an opening. Statics are exact; reactions converge under refinement while peak stresses
  at re-entrant corners do not; each bearing's resultant sits toward its inner edge; the principal
  membrane forces show the bottle-shaped struts and the tie a strut-and-tie model reads.

### Differentiable path
- Triangles take part in `solve`/`compliance` on a `ModelState`, with gradients with respect to
  positions, thickness and design-dependent triangle loads. Reverse rules for the triangle
  kernels (`AsapShellADExt`) compile in seconds even on Julia 1.10. The linear-cost Zygote group
  rules cover any node count. Enzyme (reverse through bridged rules, forward native) and Mooncake
  work through triangles: the Enzyme matrix gains a triangle case (Julia 1.13 and 1.12).

## Changed
- `FEFTable` gained triangle-load fields (`tloads`, `telem`, `tnodes`, `tscatter`) at the end;
  code that reads fields by name is unaffected.
- `ShellElement` accepts 3 or 4 nodes (it refused 3 before).
- The front-page image of the README and the docs follows the house figure style.

## Downstream
- AsapOptim: `ThicknessVariable`, `shell_volume`, `solve_structure` and `solution_tangents` on
  triangles and mixed quadrilateral/triangle meshes.

Formulations and verification: `docs/shells/DERIVATION.md` §7–8.
